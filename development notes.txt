The Korath simply presented the least contrived justification for some elements, but these are proposed alternatives:

Option 1:

 - a) (larger re-write) The flaw that allows small bits of insulation to break off. This is a flaw at the seal of the crate's door; the door clips the insulation edge and pieces break off like asbestos fibers. They go everywhere and are detectable.
 - b) (smaller re-write) The flaw that allows small bits of insulation to break off like asbestos fibers. These are inhaled by the inhabitants and leads to Cadmium poisoning as in the current version.

Option 2:
- 1) Strange sensor readings come from strange devices on the Kidnappers' ships. These are the devices that let them see the planet that they operate from.

__**Re "Hidden Systems/Wormholes":**__

Alteration to the Back-story:
1) During the Korath raids a number of Hai dissidents took a captured Korath ship and left to make their own way. To support this effort they press-ganged a number of Hai with useful skills into helping them and installed a system of holographic projectors to make the ship look like an asteroid for escape/stealth purposes.
2) This group eventually made it's way to Freedom, where an eventually botched reltionship led to an anarchist faction taking over the ship and operation. This radical group had no compunctions about enslaving necessary skills (and aliens) to advance their goals. With Hai knowledge of ideal kidnapping victims and Hai society, they went on the original kidnapping spree.
3) At length, they couldn't maintain the ship's aging systems for long, so they scoured the ship's database for ideas before discovering that a ship could hover near Geminga, beyond the reach of Hai or human sensors, if one knows safe routes and has suitable sensors.
4) Hide there and set up.
5) Many years pass.

For this purpose, Geminga would be a neutron star nova system on the edge, or just inside, of Syndicate space.
It would always be visible from the start of the game. It could either be jump drive only or accessible by a hyperdrive link that's hard to find. (So the system is always there, but it's not readily accessible to your average civilian.)

I found some time to have a bit of a look, and really at this point if you're going to do things like completely reform the Syndicate and how it's presented in-game, then a lot of these changes make sense in that context.

I will note though, that there's a central question that is begging which is "why was this not discovered beforehand?"

If all you have to do is fly close enough to a holographically hidden station to see through it - it requires a fairly substantial suspension of disbelief to justify it not having been discovered before now. Previously, this was addressed with things like the cadmium poisoning, the geodesic geocoris to serve as a lead connecting things, and the involvement of Terry as a plant inside the syndicate to deliberately mis-direct investigations. (Like, this is why she exists.)

This current plan appears to remove all of that which re-begs the central question - so it would be good for you to think of some other thing to answer it if you could.

Otherwise, my only comment is that the Hai government stuff is something a lot of people have really liked, and was really intended to show a bit of how they'd maintained stability despite the deepening cracks in their society so that those social aspects could then be utilised for future Hai/Uhai plots (especially the future possibility of reunification).
- It might need a few more ways for the player to have less reading if they're not particularly invested, but I'd implore you to keep as much of it as possible for those who are interested, and to support future plots.

As an aside - the approach in here *seems* to be shifting away from having 2 parallel stories (the main political crisis, and the potential crisis arising from the kidnappings) that Sayari is trying to resolve by getting you involved - right up until they inevitable intersect... to now instead having one more singular plot thread.
- I don't know if you've explicitly thought about that shift or if it's accidental, but I think that has the potential to make the story feel a lot weaker. So, I wanted to point it out as that should be a deliberate decision you make one way or another, not an accidental thing.
